Game UI mockup for a low-poly stylized top-down game set in dark caves. One inventory window shown at the center of a 16:9 image, over a dim low-poly cave floor with only rocks and stalagmites (no bones, skulls or ribcages anywhere in the background).
Mood and colors: dark, gloomy cave. The stone frame is warm cave brown close to #733e39, shading down to dark brown #3e2731. Brown only, never purple, slate or blue. Text plates and slot insets near-black #181425.
Style: faceted carved stone frame, flat-shaded low-poly facets with crisp angular bevels, a thin dim teal glow line (close to #0099db) along the window edge. Plain stone only: no skulls, bones or other ornaments on the frame. Light from the upper left. Matte, no gloss, no noise.
Frame for 9-slice stretching: shapes may differ only in the four corners; the edges between the corners are an even, repeating stone pattern with no unique features.
Layout: a title bar at the top with an EMPTY flat dark plate where the title text will go, a square close button at the top right with a simple cross icon, a grid of 5 columns by 3 rows of square item slots with flat dark insets, and an EMPTY flat dark info strip at the bottom. A few slots hold low-poly item icons: a bone, a skull, a red potion bottle, an iron key. Other slots are empty.
Readability: all text areas are flat, dark, evenly colored and free of decoration, ready for bright text to be added later.
Reference image: follow its stone frame, colors and layout, but do NOT copy its skull and bone corner ornaments or the ribcage in its background.
No letters, no numbers, no runes, no writing, no logos, no color swatches anywhere.
